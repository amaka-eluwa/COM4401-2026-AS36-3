## Final Hardware Recommendation

The team reviewed the workstation requirements for each employee role and selected hardware according to the tasks, performance requirements and budget allocated to each role.

The final workstation recommendations are based on the workstation builds recorded in the project database.

| Role | Quantity | Hardware Cost per Workstation | Total Role Hardware Cost | Budget Position |

The complete hardware refresh costs **£23,666.80** for 12 workstations.

The overall hardware budget is **£25,450**, leaving **£1,783.20** remaining within the allocated budget.

Software and operating system licensing are managed separately and are not included in the hardware total.

## Overall Budget Review

The recommended hardware costs are calculated from the individual hardware items recorded in the database.

The total cost for each role is calculated by multiplying the cost of one workstation by the required number of staff.

The final calculation is:

- Administrative Assistant: **3 × £779.69 = £2,339.07**
- Project Manager: **2 × £986.30 = £1,972.60**
- Architect / CAD Designer: **5 × £2,465.99 = £12,329.95**
- 3D Visualisation Specialist: **2 × £3,512.59 = £7,025.18**

**Total hardware refresh: £23,666.80**

The hardware refresh therefore remains within the overall **£25,450 hardware budget**, with **£1,783.20 remaining**.

<br>

## Ethical and Professional Considerations

Responsible purchasing was considered when selecting the workstation hardware.

We selected hardware according to the requirements of each employee role rather than giving every employee the same specification. This helps avoid unnecessary expenditure while ensuring that employees performing more demanding technical work have suitable equipment.

We also considered value for money. Higher-cost components were selected where their performance was relevant to the role, such as the dedicated RTX 5070 graphics cards for CAD and 3D visualisation work and 64GB of RAM for the 3D Visualisation Specialist.

We also considered the available budgets when making purchasing recommendations. Each workstation remains within its allocated per-unit budget, and the complete hardware refresh remains within the overall £25,450 hardware budget.

The final workstation recommendations provide different levels of hardware performance according to the requirements of each role.
